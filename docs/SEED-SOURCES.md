# Suggested initial evidence sources

The app is intentionally source-agnostic. For an AI data provenance demo, start with a small, legally reusable corpus and preserve each canonical URL, publisher, version/date, and source text exactly as ingested.

Suggested subject areas:

- AI data provenance and lineage
- NIST AI Risk Management Framework material relevant to data governance and traceability
- EU AI Act provisions relevant to data governance, documentation, traceability, and record keeping
- public technical or policy material whose reuse conditions you have verified

Do not treat a public URL as permission to redistribute its full text. Verify reuse/licensing conditions before copying source text into a distributable dataset.
