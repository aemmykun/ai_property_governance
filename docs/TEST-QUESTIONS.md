# Minimal test questions

Use these after ingesting a small AI data provenance corpus:

1. What is AI data provenance?
2. How is data provenance different from data lineage?
3. Why does provenance matter for RAG systems?
4. What information should a provenance record contain?
5. How can provenance help investigate an incorrect AI answer?
6. What does the evidence say about source versioning?
7. What does the evidence say about transformations applied to data?
8. Why are source URLs alone insufficient proof of what influenced an answer?
9. Which retrieved source supports your answer most strongly?
10. If the corpus does not support a claim, say that the evidence is insufficient.
